Title: Practice Streak

Notes / Description:
Built a reusable low-stakes practice league. A coach enrolls member addresses and opens sequential rounds, members submit their own bounded session logs, GenLayer consensus labels each QUALIFIES, PARTIAL, or REJECTED, peers may challenge another member once, and deterministic 2/1/0 scoring updates current and longest streaks. It uses no sensors, tokens, or high-stakes decisions.
